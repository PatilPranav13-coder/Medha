**Notice version:** `2026-11-27-v1`  
**Purpose:** provide the Medha public beta assistant, keep your account secure, and let you manage the information you choose to save.

Medha collects your email address and password verifier to create and protect your account. It stores the conversations you send, assistant replies, feedback you submit, and any memories or files you choose to save so those features work across your signed-in sessions.

When you select web search, Medha sends the search query to the operator-configured search provider. The beta is configured for a local model server; chat text is not sent to a hosted model API by default. The operator may change this configuration and must update this notice before changing the processing purpose.

You can export your account data, delete conversations, and delete your account from the product. Account deletion removes the account's database records and stored uploads. Operational event logs contain counts and event names, not message text, and are retained for the configured period (30 days by default).

Medha is an experimental public beta. Answers are AI-generated, can be inaccurate, and are not professional advice. Do not use it for emergencies or decisions that require a qualified professional.

By checking the box at signup, you confirm that you have read this notice and agree to this processing for the beta. The application stores the notice version and timestamp with your account. You may withdraw by deleting your account. Contact **`GRIEVANCE_EMAIL`** for privacy questions or grievances; the operator must set this environment variable to a monitored address before launch.

This notice is a product draft. It does not claim compliance with any law and needs review by the owner and a qualified lawyer.
